On October 26 the official marketplace listed the wrong kind of blank check.

Six Dogecoin transactions spent 264 Doginal Dogs out of wallets that had them listed on market.doginaldogs.com. Every input carried a seller signature of type 0x82 — SIGHASH_NONE|ANYONECANPAY. That signature commits to the input and to nothing else. It does not lock the price. It does not lock the recipient. Anyone who could grab the signature could spend the dog to any address they chose and pay nothing. Community archives put roughly 201 of the dogs at one recipient address and 63 on the way to miner coinbase. The marketplace site put up a notice that trading was temporarily paused.

That is the ugly room. Not a floor screenshot. Not a Spaces highlight. A design choice in the listing flow that turned every open listing into an unlocked door.

## The tickets, then the calls

The team opened a support ticket system in Discord. Holders who lost dogs filed. Then the leadership started contacting people one at a time.

Christian Barker (Bark), Damien Galvin (Shield), and David Chaboki (Shibo) pulled replacements out of their own personal holdings. Trait-matched where they could. Comparable swaps where the holder preferred something else. In some cases upgrades. The company statement put the estimated value covered above $500,000. No outside fund. No insurance claim that we have seen reported. Personal bags.

Bark’s line in the announcement was plain: “A true community is there for you when times get tough.”

Holders posted the other side of those calls. Personal outreach. Matching. A couple of surprises that were better than the original. The process ran from the late-October sweeps to the early-November statement. Affected holders who had not been reached yet were told to open a ticket through the official channels.

That is the concrete room. Not a press release about “community values.” A Discord ticket queue and three people going into their own collections so other people did not stay empty-handed.

## Why this is the same story as the rest of the build

Doginal Dogs has never had outside venture money. The free mint in January 2024 was covered by the team. The events — more than twenty of them, self-funded, including the record DDNYC in September — came out of the same pocket. The Gary plush sold out its 10,000-unit run. Rise of the Pack, the 111-card hand-drawn Legends set, reached holders in early October and is already on kitchen tables and in graded charity auctions. The digital beta sits at ddltcg.com. DD Miami is still on the calendar for December 7–9, with the first official DDL championship at Collectors Club on the 8th.

All of that was built in public, paid for in public, and shown in person. The marketplace pause and the replacement round is the same operating system under stress. When the on-chain piece broke in a way that hit holders, the response was not “sorry, the market is the market.” It was “we will match what you lost from what we hold.”

Most collections do not do that. Most teams issue a statement about security reviews and move on. The pack has spent two years proving that the dogs are the foundation and everything else — cards, events, radio, agents — gets built on top by people who actually show up. Making holders whole after a sweep is just the next receipt.

## What the pack does next

The thief wallet has not moved the dogs in any meaningful way that has been reported. The marketplace remains paused while protections are upgraded. Holders still on the old listing signatures were told to move remaining dogs to fresh wallets. The on-chain facts are public; the independent archive at doginaldogs.info tracked the six transactions, the 0x82 signatures, and the flow.

None of that changes the December calendar. Miami is still the next room. Early-bird tickets and swag packages sold out earlier. The championship is still on the schedule. People are still ripping packs, running community tables, and posting pulls. The same energy that filled Chelsea in September is pointed at Wynwood in December.

The lesson is not that nothing bad can happen on-chain. It is that when it does, the people who built the brand out of their own time and money treat the holders like the rest of the product. You do not get to claim family only on the green days.

We keep shipping. The dogs stay on Dogecoin. The cards stay on the table. The next hotel is already booked.

Until the next room.
