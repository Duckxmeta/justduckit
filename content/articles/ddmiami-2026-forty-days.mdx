Forty days out the official account posted the number and the hotel link. Rooms at the Arlo Wynwood are limited. Lock yours in. That is the message that hit the timeline this morning.

DDMIAMI 2026 runs December 7–9. The schedule has been public since the twentieth. Tickets and merch packages sold out. The rooms are the last piece still moving. This is not a soft launch. It is the next room the pack occupies after New York and Vegas.

## The hotel is the base

Higher Ground at the Arlo opens day one at 10 a.m. Swag Drop and Welcome Party run until 5. That is where the Dog Pod sits — a production studio built for the event so creators can cut content on site instead of in a hallway. VIP Dinner at 7. Kick-Off on the rooftop from 8 to 11. The hotel is not just lodging. It is the first set of rooms.

Day three closes the same building. Hangover Hangout 9 a.m. to noon at Higher Ground. The pattern is deliberate. Start in the hotel, finish in the hotel, put the loudest night somewhere else.

## Collectors Club is the table

December 8 is the card day. Rip & Sip at Collectors Club in Wynwood from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. That session is also the DDL Miami Championship — the first official in-person tournament for Doginal Dogs Legends. Rise of the Pack left the screen at DDNYC. In Miami it gets a bracket, a title, and prizes that include ultra-rare promo cards, rare dogs, and boxes of the set.

Collectors Club already runs an on-site grading lab. The room is built for people who treat the cards as both game and object. The championship is not a side activity. It is the middle day of the flagship.

## The Vessel is the closer

After the second VIP Dinner at 7, boarding starts for Dogs at Sea. The Vessel is a floating venue on Biscayne Bay that opened in 2025. Boarding 7 to 8. Voyage from 8 until midnight. That is the finale the schedule lists. Three days, multiple venues, one city. The yacht is the last room before the hangover hangout the next morning.

Christian Barker called it the most ambitious event yet. The public schedule matches the claim: hotel takeover, creator studio, championship table, floating closer. The pattern from prior events holds — physical rooms first, price and attention follow.

## What the forty days are for

The timeline has been counting down since the sixty-day mark. Schedule dropped. Tickets gone. Now the hotel note. The work between now and December 7 is the same work that filled Dream Downtown: people who already treat the pack as the place they show up. Spaces still run. Pulls still happen. Carolina pop-outs and local tournaments keep the cards moving. Miami is the concentration point.

Rooms are limited because the hotel is part of the product, not an afterthought. Staying there is recommended, not required. The people who lock a room early are the ones who treat the three days as one continuous block instead of a series of tickets.

## The lesson that keeps repeating

Free mint on Dogecoin. Hand-drawn cards. Self-funded rooms in cities that already have every other event. The difference is still the same one the pack keeps proving: show up in the actual room. The phone keeps the culture alive between events. The hotel, the club, the yacht, and the hangover hangout are where the culture gets a body count.

Forty days. The schedule is public. The rooms are moving. The championship has a date and a venue. The rest is whether the pack fills them the way it filled the last ones.

See you in Wynwood.
