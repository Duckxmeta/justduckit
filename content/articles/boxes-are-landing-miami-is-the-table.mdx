The hotel lights are off. The boxes are not.

On October 6, [@DDLTCG](https://x.com/DDLTCG) posted the line the pack has been waiting on since Chelsea: preorders have all been shipped and are currently being received. Rip the packs. Build the decks. Get ready for the first official DDLTCG Championship, which kicks off in December at DD Miami. One legend will rule them all. The follow-up link points at the event page. Bark's reply was two words. Epic art.

That is the story. Not another recap of the pool deck. The product is in transit, and the next room already has a date on it.

## What actually moved

DDNYC was the debut. Rise of the Pack hit the tables, the first in-person tournament ran, and the timeline filled with pulls. We already wrote that chapter. What changed this week is logistics plus a named championship.

The official post does not invent a print run or a prize pool. It says the preorder wave is out and people are opening it. That matches the kitchen-table posts that started before the announcement — holders talking about boxes already shipped, kids hunting a Mama Bark, pack members adding another box off the limited restock Shibo flagged on October 3. Bark asked the timeline this morning if the boxes had landed, and told people to show the pulls.

A TCG is not real when the press release says it is real. It is real when a porch in the middle of nowhere has a mailer on it and somebody is arguing about lanes before dinner.

DD Miami sits on the calendar as December 7–9. The event page calls it a three-day takeover — events, food, drinks, art, music — and lists a DDL Miami Championship with an apply button next to Dog Talk. Hotel partner booking is still marked coming soon. Tickets have been the front of the shop for weeks. None of that is a rumor we have to dress up. The official account tied the first championship to that week. The page already has a slot for it.

## From inscription to a sleeve on the counter

This is the free-mint thesis with a shipping label.

The dogs started as 10,000 hand-curated pixel inscriptions on Dogecoin. No one needed a card shop to understand a pfp. Legends asks the harder question: will the same pack sit down with 111 hand-drawn cards, build a 40-card deck, and care who wins in December?

The digital side is still the practice room. [ddltcg.com](https://ddltcg.com) runs the beta — Wizard, Crown, Zombie, Bow, Pirate, bots, PvP, daily tournaments. That loop matters because a rulebook argument in Miami is a bad first date with the game. The physical side is the object a cousin can hold without a wallet. Preorders leaving the warehouse is the bridge between those two doors.

Most NFT drops peak at the mint and then beg the timeline to care. Legends sold the preorder, debuted in a sold-out room, kept moving on secondary after the bags left Chelsea, took a limited restock, and is now telling holders the mail is the next move. That is a product cycle, not a content cycle.

We are not going to fake box counts. The October 3 restock was called super limited. The shop has listed Rise of the Pack booster boxes. Whether that listing is still live by the time this goes up is a screenshot job, not a sentence we get to invent. The direction is enough. People who missed the first window got another crack. People who already paid are being told the cardboard is in motion.

## The championship is the point of the cardboard

A chase card in a binder is a collectible. A chase card in a deck at a named event is a game.

DDNYC proved the pack would rip in public. The first official championship is a different ask. Somebody has to apply. Somebody has to show up with a deck that is not a meme. Somebody has to lose on camera, or at least in a room full of people who will remember the lane. The official line — one legend will rule them all — is flavor. The structure under it is the part that matters: a date, a city, an apply path, and a set that is supposed to be in hands before the flight.

That is how culture leaves the group chat. Card shops and holiday tables do not care what an inscription is. They care if the art is finished, the rules hold, and a room will crown a winner. Miami is the next room that can do that in public.

Rural Tennessee does not get a pass on the homework either. If the box lands on a gravel driveway, the job is the same as a condo in Brickell. Sleeve it. Learn the lanes. Do not wait for a moderator to tell us the meta. The pack has spent years showing up in Spaces and hotels. The championship is the version of showing up that requires a deck list.

## What we do between the porch and December

Three moves, none of them a lecture.

Rip what arrived and post the pull. Bark already asked. The official account already told people to build decks. A quiet binder does not train anybody for Miami.

Play the digital beta before the ego gets involved. Bots are cheaper than a bad loss in front of the pack. Five classes is enough flavor to pick a lane and stop theory-crafting in the replies.

If Miami is the table we want, treat the apply button like a door, not a graphic. The event page has the championship listed. Tickets and the Box of Awesomeness have been the shop's front end. Hotel blocks are not locked on the page yet. That is ops, and ops slip when everybody assumes somebody else booked the room.

We do not need a new origin story for any of this. The dogs were the entry. Legends is the object. DD Miami is the next room with a winner.

The boxes are landing. The championship has a month on the calendar. One legend does not get crowned in a quote tweet.

Proof the pulls. Build the deck. Then say we are going.
