The timeline has been quiet on the big candles and loud on the small ones. Black Friday posts from the official account. A Santa Gary promo card that drops into carts over a certain number. Community notes that Miami is close enough to start thinking about luggage.

Twenty days out the official account said it plain. Now we are inside fifteen. DD Miami runs December 7 to 9. That is the next room.

## The rooms that already have names

Day one opens at Higher Ground at the Arlo with the swag drop and welcome. A VIP dinner. A rooftop kick-off. Day two is the one the card people have been marking: Rip & Sip at Collectors Club, which doubles as the DDL Miami Championship. In-person. Bracket. Prizes. Then another dinner and Dogs at Sea on The Vessel out on Biscayne Bay. Day three is the hangover hangout back at the hotel.

Schedule is still marked subject to change. That is how these things work until the doors open. What is not subject to change is the pattern we already saw in New York and Vegas: the cards leave the screen and hit a table, the merch leaves the shop and hits a body, and the people who only know each other from Spaces finally stand in the same room.

Dog Pod is listed as a creator studio on site. That is not a booth. That is a place to make the content while the event is happening instead of trying to recreate it later from a hotel lobby.

## What the shop is still doing

The Black Friday sale went live with the full inventory one last time. Limited stock. A free Santa Gary promo card on qualifying carts. Not sold separately. While supplies last. People posted the hoodies and the boxes. Some of that product is pickup-only in Miami. The Box of Awesomeness is made to order and has to be collected in person.

That is the free-mint-to-IRL pipeline in one checkout. The inscription is permanent on DOGE. The hoodie is cotton. The card is paper stock you can sleeve and shuffle. When the championship runs at Collectors Club, the decks people bring will have been printed, shipped, and practiced with long before anyone steps off a plane.

## Why the plane ticket still decides it

Daily Spaces keep the mics on when the charts go quiet. That part is real. You can hear the same voices every morning. You can trade in the replies. None of that puts a deck on a table or a face next to a name you have only typed.

The championship is the concrete test. Someone will climb the bracket in a room that smells like card sleeves and hotel AC. Someone will lose a game they practiced for weeks. Both of those things only happen if the people show up. The yacht does not sail for a Discord. The hangover hangout does not have a virtual queue.

We already know the lesson from the last flagship. Physical presence still does the work the timeline cannot. A free mint that fills a hotel is not a miracle. It is years of the same people keeping the rooms open until the rooms become buildings.

## What we are actually packing

Sleeves. A deck list that has been tested. The hoodie that will get worn in the photos. The names of people we have talked to for months and never stood next to. A phone charged enough to record the round we lose and the round we win.

The official page still lists tickets and the hotel block at the Arlo. Some of the merch packages sold through. The championship application is open. None of that is a promise that every seat is easy. It is the opposite. The seats that matter fill because the pack treats the calendar like a real date instead of a content calendar.

Fifteen days is enough time to book the flight if you have not. It is not enough time to invent a reason to stay home. The next room is already named. The cards are already printed. The only open question is who walks in with a deck and who watches the bracket from the group chat.

We get on the plane or we read the recap. That part has not changed since the last one.
