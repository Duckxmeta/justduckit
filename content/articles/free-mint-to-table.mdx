## The free mint was the entry ticket

January 11, 2024. Ten thousand pixel dogs. Free. No VC. No paid push. The project covered the fees. Most people took two and left. A year and a half later the same collection is still the loudest thing on Dogecoin inscriptions and one of the few NFT projects that did not bleed out when the rest of the market went quiet.

Rankings move. Trust scores move. Floor screenshots age. What does not move is the fact that a free-mint community is still shipping physical product in October 2026. Rise of the Pack pre-orders have been arriving worldwide for weeks. Official channels highlighted the rips. Community members posted master-set pages. Someone is already holding a Gem Mint 10. That is not a chart. That is cardboard on a kitchen table.

## Hand-drawn still matters

Crypto is still full of generative sludge. Rise of the Pack went the other direction. One hundred eleven original cards drawn by the team. Not a template with a logo. Not AI fill. The chase cards have weight. The holos catch light. People are sleeving them, grading them, and putting them in custom master-set pages instead of just posting the pull and moving on.

That is the difference between a drop and a product. A drop ends when the mint sells out. A product keeps generating rooms. Trivia Spaces. Bracket tournaments. Play-table builds. The first official championship is already on the calendar for December in Miami. The same pack that claimed free dogs is now organizing the rooms that will decide who holds the title.

## The ranking is the byproduct

We can talk about number-one spots and trust scores. Those numbers exist because the community kept showing up after the free mint. Daily Spaces. Multi-city events. Merch that actually ships. A TCG that people are willing to learn. The floor can be whatever the market decides on any given day. The rooms are harder to fake.

A free mint that becomes a lifestyle brand is rare. A free mint that becomes a lifestyle brand *and* a competitive tabletop game is rarer. The official post is correct that the speed was unexpected. The part that matters more is that the speed produced something people can still hold in October.

The free-mint story is real. The number-one claim is on the timeline. The proof is the cardboard that is still landing and the people who keep organizing the rooms around it.
