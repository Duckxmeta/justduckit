The official account posted it plain this morning. DDMIAMI 2026 is in two weeks. The Dogs are about to take on Vice City.

That is not a teaser. The full schedule has been public since October. Tickets and the bigger packages sold out. The rooms are already claimed. What we have now is the final stretch of prep before the pack occupies Miami the same way it occupied Chelsea.

We already know what a Doginal Dogs event looks like when it works. Merch first. Cards on tables. Conversations that turn into work. A roof or a deck that actually functions. Hospitality treated like product. DDMIAMI is the next one on the calendar, and the calendar is not soft.

## The three days are concrete

December 7 opens at Higher Ground at the Arlo Hotel. Swag Drop and Welcome Party from 10AM to 5PM. A ticketed VIP Dinner at 7PM. Kick-Off on the Arlo rooftop from 8PM to 11PM. Dog Pod is there too — the on-site production studio so creators can film in the room instead of chasing content after the fact.

December 8 is the centerpiece. Rip & Sip at Collectors Club in Wynwood from 11AM to 3PM. That block doubles as the DDL Miami Championship, an in-person tournament for Doginal Dogs Legends. Prizes on the line. Then another VIP Dinner at 7PM, and boarding The Vessel for Dogs at Sea. The yacht runs 8PM to midnight on Biscayne Bay.

December 9 closes with the Hangover Hangout, 9AM to noon, back at Higher Ground. Same shape as New York. The programmed blocks end. The follow-up does not.

Collectors Club is a trading-card venue with a grading lab on site. The Vessel is a floating space that opened last year. Arlo anchors the hotel side. None of this is abstract. The venues exist. The times are published. The official page has the map.

## The championship is the point of the weekend

Rise of the Pack left the screen at DDNYC. Boxes hit doorsteps. Cards started moving on eBay. Digital beta is already playable. The Miami championship is the next proof that the game is not a drop with a schedule. It is a table people sit at in public.

Qualifiers and daily tournaments have been running. The finance show this morning even flagged instructions for the DDMiami finals. That is the loop. Spaces and daily grind build the skill. The Wynwood room turns the skill into a result other people can watch.

We do not need to invent pull rates or claim the prize pool. The existence of the in-person championship is enough. A free-mint collection that produces a hand-drawn TCG and then stages a live tournament for it is doing the thing most projects only talk about. On-chain identity becomes cardboard becomes competition becomes culture that travels without a seed phrase.

## Two weeks is a short runway

Chelsea taught us the obvious lesson. You either get on the plane or you watch the recap from the group chat. Miami is the same test with different weather and a yacht instead of a roof.

The people who are already locked in are posting about rooming at the Arlo, bringing master sets, trading grading notes in person, and grinding the daily tournaments. The people still deciding have two weeks to decide. Early-bird pricing is gone. The packages moved. What remains is whether you show up with a deck or you do not.

Dog Pod matters for the same reason the merch table mattered in New York. Culture has to be wearable and filmable in the room or it stays a profile picture. Creators who film on site produce the evidence that the weekend happened. The rest of the pack sees it later. The people in the room already lived it.

## What the free mint actually bought

The dogs started as inscriptions on Dogecoin. Ten thousand pixel characters. No paid mint. The rooms filled because people treated the collection like characters instead of inventory. Legends was the physical extension. DDMIAMI is the next room that treats the physical extension as the main event.

Most cycles peak at the drop and go quiet. This one keeps booking the next hotel. New York ran. Vegas ran. Miami is the one that sits two weeks out with a published championship and a boat on the schedule. That is not marketing copy. That is operational proof that the community still wants the same block of days in the same city.

We are not writing a travel guide. We are noting that the pack keeps choosing to occupy real rooms after the free mint closed. The secondary market on the cards is one receipt. The sold-out Miami tickets are another. Both exist because the same people care about both layers.

## Two weeks from now the tables are set

If you have a deck, sleeve the extras and decide which cards you are willing to trade in Wynwood. If you are still learning the lanes, the digital beta is open and the daily tournaments are still running. If you are building content, Dog Pod is the room that exists so you do not have to invent the footage later.

The official post is short on purpose. Two weeks. Vice City. The rest of the details have been public long enough that the only variable left is whether we show up.

The dogs started on-chain. The packs are already moving. Miami is next.
