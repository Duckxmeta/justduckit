Thirty days. The official account posted the number this morning with a palm tree and the link. DDMIAMI 2026 runs December 7 to 9. Tickets and the Box of Awesomeness packages sold out weeks ago. The secondary market is already moving tickets. The decks are already built. The flights are already booked.

This is not a hype cycle. This is the calendar catching up to the work the pack has been doing since the free mint in January 2024.

## The rooms are concrete

Day one opens at Higher Ground at the Arlo Hotel in Wynwood. Swag drop and welcome party from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. That is the same rhythm as New York: merch first, then the agenda. Culture has to be wearable or it stays a profile picture.

New this year is Dog Pod. An on-site production studio inside the welcome party. Creators can shoot, record, and collab without leaving the room. The pack already makes content every day on Spaces and X. Miami gives them a physical booth with lights and a backdrop instead of a phone in a hotel hallway.

VIP dinner at 7. Kick-off on the Arlo rooftop from 8 to 11. Same skyline energy that made the PHD roof in Chelsea feel like a movie, just with warmer air and different palm trees.

Day two is the one the card players have been waiting for. Rip & Sip at Collectors Club from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. doubles as the first official DDL Miami Championship. Rise of the Pack left the screen at DDNYC. It hit tables in Charlotte in late October. Now it gets a full bracket, prizes, and an AGS grading lab in the same building. Players compete. Everyone else rips packs and watches. That is how a TCG stops being merch and becomes the game.

VIP dinner again at 7. Then boarding for Dogs at Sea on The Vessel. The yacht sails Biscayne Bay from 8 p.m. to midnight. Four hours on the water with the lights. The Vessel has already hosted Dom Pérignon dinners and Swim Week runways. The pack is using it for the same reason it used Dream Downtown: the room has to match the energy the community already brings.

Day three is the Hangover Hangout back at Higher Ground from 9 a.m. to noon. Coffee. Names. Follow-ups. The part that usually dies in a group chat if nobody treats it like operations.

## What the sold-out list actually means

Early-bird tickets and swag packages moved in September. The official shop shows the admission ticket sold out. Holders on X are already talking about secondary tickets and who is bringing extra decks. That is the same pattern after DDNYC: the product left the hotel and found a market. The difference this time is the countdown is still running and the rooms are already full.

The team has run more than twenty self-funded events. Gary the plush sold out ten thousand units. Rise of the Pack boxes started landing on doorsteps in early October. Graded cards went into charity auctions. Community tournaments happened without a central organizer. Miami is the next room that proves the pattern holds when the city changes and the stakes go up.

Bark called this the most ambitious event yet. The schedule backs that up. A creator studio, a championship at a real card shop with same-day grading, and a yacht close. None of that is free. None of it comes from a venture round. The same people who replaced stolen dogs from their own collections in late October are the ones booking the yacht. That is the through-line.

## The TCG is the thread

Legends is not an afterthought. The championship sits in the middle of the weekend for a reason. Holders started playing the moment the boxes arrived. Digital tables at ddltcg.com let people practice before they fly. Physical tables at Collectors Club let them prove the practice. The first official title is on the line in Miami. That changes how people build decks between now and December 8.

The same holders who are counting down the days are the ones who already ran a tournament in Charlotte with no team required. Just tables and decks. Miami formalizes what the pack already started doing on its own.

## Thirty days is a long time if the work is already done

Most communities quiet down when the market does. The timeline stays active here. Daily Spaces. Pack openings. Merch unboxings. The official account boosting plain posts from holders who just got a box or just put their dog next to themselves in a photo. The 30-day post this morning is the latest in a line that has not broken.

The work between now and December is simple. Finish the deck. Book the hotel block at Arlo if you still can. Open the ticket if your dog got swept in October and you have not heard back yet. Show up with a name that is not just a handle. The rooms are already reserved. The only variable left is whether the people who talk about the pack online actually walk into them.

DDNYC proved the model in Chelsea. Vegas proved it in a different climate. Miami is the next test. Thirty days. The calendar is public. The decks are building. The pack is already there.
