The official account dropped the line this morning: DDMIAMI 2026 is in 10 days. Who’s ready for our best event to date.

That is not a teaser. That is the schedule going live on the calendar. December 7–9. Higher Ground at Arlo. Collectors Club in Wynwood. The Vessel on Biscayne Bay. Swag drop. VIP dinners. Rip & Sip that doubles as the first official DDL Miami Championship. Dogs at Sea after dark.

We already know what a Doginal Dogs room feels like. DDNYC filled Dream Downtown for three days. Vegas shut a block. The pattern is the same every time: the timeline builds the culture, the hotel proves it was real.

## The rooms that matter

Day one opens at Higher Ground, Arlo Hotel. Swag Drop and Welcome Party from 10 to 5. The Dog Pod is in the building — an on-site creator studio so people can film while the line is still moving. VIP Dinner at 7. Kick-Off on the rooftop until 11. Same energy as the Beach at Dream Downtown: density you cannot fake with a guest list. Merch first. Conversations second. Nobody needs a keynote to know they are in the right building.

Day two is the one the card people have been waiting on. Rip & Sip at Collectors Club, 11 to 3. That session is also the DDL Miami Championship. Online round-robin the week before, top 16 advance, then face-to-face in Wynwood. Prizes are real: ultra-rare Gary promo, rare Dogs, boxes of Rise of the Pack. After that, second VIP Dinner, then boarding The Vessel for Dogs at Sea. 8 to midnight on the water. The schedule calls it the finale. We know what that means. The night that turns a hotel weekend into a story people still tell in Spaces six weeks later.

Day three closes with the Hangover Hangout back at Higher Ground, 9 to noon. The part that usually dissolves into “we should do this again.” The part that only works if people already traded numbers on the roof the night before.

Tickets and swag packages sold out earlier. That is the receipt. The official site still lists the schedule. The championship application is still open for the people who locked their lists. The rest of us are already packing.

## Why the championship is the plot

Rise of the Pack left the screen at DDNYC. Packs hit tables on day one. A tournament ran under the lights. Cards showed up on eBay with real bids. That was the proof that a free-mint pixel collection could become something you hold, sleeve, and play with your kid.

Miami is the next room for that game. The first official championship. Not a side event. The session that decides who has the best deck in the pack right now. Online qualifiers already running. Top 16 in person. The same set that sold out its preorder is the set that will crown a champion while everyone else rips packs and watches.

We built Legends so the dogs could leave the wallet and sit on a table. The championship is the moment that table has a bracket and prizes that are not screenshots. Collectors Club has an on-site grading lab. People will be holding cards that were drawn by the team, not generated. That distinction still matters.

## Showing up is still the product

Most of this culture lives in a phone. Daily Spaces. Floor posts. Thanksgiving notes from the official account that say the same thing every year: we are grateful for every dog that keeps showing up no matter what. The note landed yesterday. The countdown landed today.

The Spaces keep the rooms open when the hotel is closed. The hotel is what proves the Spaces were not just noise. Bark said it when the schedule dropped: every time we plan an event we aim to go above and beyond the last one. That is not marketing copy. That is the operating rule. New York. Vegas. Now Miami. Same pack. Bigger rooms.

Ten days is not a long time. It is long enough to book the flight if you have not already. Long enough to lock a deck list if you are playing. Long enough to decide whether you are watching the recap from the group chat or standing in the line for swag when the doors open at Higher Ground.

The Dogs never quit. The official account said that weeks ago while the broader market was red. The floor in DOGE stayed relatively steady. The daily rooms stayed open. The next physical room is already on the calendar.

We will be in Miami. The pack will fill the rooms. The championship will crown someone. Dogs at Sea will sail. The rest is just logistics.

See you in ten days.
