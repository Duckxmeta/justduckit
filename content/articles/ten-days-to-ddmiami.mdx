Yesterday the official account posted the clock. Ten days. Palm trees in the graphic. Link to the events page. The replies started filling with people saying they are coming, people asking who else is locked in, and the usual quiet ones who will just show up with a hat on.

DDNYC already happened. We have the recap. The roof was a movie. The cards left the screen and hit tables. That was the proof of concept. Miami is the next room.

December 7 to 9. Higher Ground at the Arlo for the swag and the welcome. Collectors Club for the Rip & Sip that doubles as the first official DDL Miami Championship. The Vessel on Biscayne Bay for the night that does not fit in a hotel. Hangover hangout on the last morning. The schedule has been public since October. The tickets cleared earlier. The conversation is now about who is actually in the room.

## The championship is not a side quest

The digital beta has been live at ddltcg.com/play for weeks. Tutorial, bots, PvP. Forty life, forty-card decks, five lanes, mana to ten. Same spine as the physical Rise of the Pack set. People have been grinding it in the Spaces and the group chats.

December 8 turns that into a bracket. Online round-robin the week before, top sixteen in person at Collectors Club from 11 to 3. Prizes include the ultra-rare Gary promo, boxes, and a rare Doginal Dog for first. That is not a panel. That is people sitting across from each other with sleeves on the table and a clock running.

We already saw what that looks like in New York. The tournament was not the loudest part of the night, but it was the part that proved the cards were a game and not just product. Miami is the next test. Same decks, different city, more water.

## Rooms, not feeds

Most of the year the pack lives in Spaces, in sales bots, in people posting their dog next to their actual dog. That work is real. It keeps the floor from collapsing when the broader market is soft. It keeps the radio on. It keeps the physical pieces moving.

It is still not the same as the line for merch at 10 a.m. or the argument about a pull on a boat at midnight. The official posts keep saying the same thing: the ones who keep showing up. The Thanksgiving note was plain. The countdown posts are plain. The product is the room.

DDNYC proved a free-mint pixel collection could fill a TAO hotel for three days. Miami is the next city on the list. New York, Vegas, now this. The pattern is not complicated. Put the pack in the same building and the rest of the internet becomes secondary.

## What the next ten days are for

If you are going, the work is logistics. Flight, hotel block at the Mia Wynwood if you want the official recommendation, sleeves for the championship if you signed up, the merch you already bought so you can spot each other on the sidewalk. The conversations that turn into the next six months of work usually happen between the programmed blocks, not during them.

If you are not going, the work is still the same one we keep running into. The digital game is free to open. The Spaces are still on. The sales bot is still posting. Showing up in the rooms you can reach still counts. The people who only exist in the replies will watch the recap later.

We have watched this community treat hospitality like product for a year. Rooms, timing, merch that people actually wear outside the event, a championship that has real prizes. The pixel dogs started as inscriptions. The pack turned them into something you can hold and something you can sit across from. Miami is the next place that happens in public.

Ten days. The clock is public. The assignment has not changed.

See you in the room or see you in the recap.
