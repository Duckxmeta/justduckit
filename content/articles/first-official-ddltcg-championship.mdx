The form is up. December 9. Collectors Club in Miami. 11am to 3pm. Rips & Sips. One champion leaves with the table.

That is the first official DDLTCG Championship. @DDLTCG posted the full structure on October 24. Signup form linked. Online round-robin the week before. Top 16 advance to the IRL faceoff. No more "we should run a real one someday." The path is public.

## How the bracket gets built

Anyone who signs up gets the online round-robin in the week leading up to the event. Top 16 walk into Collectors Club on December 9. One of them is crowned.

This sits on top of the digital beta and the daily play the pack has already been running. The beta taught the rules. Rise of the Pack put physical cards in hands — 111 hand-drawn cards built for 40-card decks. Now there is a published path from screen practice to a felt table with real stakes.

Prizes from the official post:

1st place: Ultra rare Gary promo card, a rare Doginal Dog, and 10 boxes of Doginal Dogs Legends: Rise of the Pack.

2nd place: Ultra rare Gary promo card and 5 boxes.

3rd place: 5 boxes.

That mix keeps the original collection in the conversation. The Dogs are still the scarce inscription. The cards are the game. The championship is how both show up in the same room.

## Why the room matters

DDNYC put packs on tables and ran early tournament energy in the hotel. DDMiami has been on the calendar for weeks. This announcement turns the Miami stop into a competitive crown instead of another meetup with merch.

Collectors Club is a trading card venue in Wynwood. That is intentional. The game is being asked to stand up in a place that already knows how to run tables, grade cards, and host collectors — not just a hotel lobby with a side table.

The pack has spent the last stretch saying the future runs past pure screen life. Physical product. Rooms. People who show up and play. A championship with a qualifier path is how that sentence gets a bracket and a date.

## Digital still does the work

The free-to-play digital beta at ddltcg.com stays the practice table. Tutorial, bots, PvP, daily tournaments. 40-card decks, max three copies, five classes, 40 HP heroes, mana rising to 10, five lanes. The physical set is what people sleeve and argue about. The championship is what turns daily play into a season with an endpoint.

Nobody needs to pretend the meta is solved. The point is the structure is now visible. Sign up, grind the online week, or watch the top 16 walk into Miami and settle it on felt.

## What we take from it

Same spine the pack has been running. Sharper because there is a crown on the calendar.

1. Structure beats vibes. Daily digital play is practice. A published path to an IRL top 16 is a season with a date.

2. The scarce original collection is still in the prize mix. A rare Doginal Dog next to the promo and the boxes keeps the origin of the pack in the championship conversation.

3. Miami is the next room that has a crown on the schedule. The online week is the filter. December 9 is the receipt.

The form is the entry. The digital beta is still open for reps. December 9 is when one deck gets called champion at Collectors Club in Miami.

Register if the deck is ready. Sleeve the chase. The table is set.
