The cards left Chelsea. Now the table is headed south.

On October 24 the official @DDLTCG account posted the details for the first official DDLTCG Championship. It runs December 9 from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Collectors Club in Miami, folded into the Rip & Sip block of DDMIAMI. All participants who sign up get an online round-robin the week before. Top 16 advance to the in-person faceoff. One of them leaves with the title.

That is not another after-party. That is the game finding its room.

## What the prize table actually looks like

First place takes an Ultra Rare Gary promo card, a rare Doginal Dog, and 10 boxes of Rise of the Pack. Second gets the Gary promo and five boxes. Third gets five boxes. The product is the prize. No sponsor logo, no gift card, no abstract token. The same boxes that sold out the preorder and shipped to doorsteps are what the winner walks away with.

Collectors Club is a trading-card venue in Wynwood. It already runs an on-site grading lab. The championship sits inside an existing card room instead of a hotel ballroom that gets cleared for the next conference. Pack members who have been ripping, grading, and building master sets will walk into a place that already speaks the language of sleeves and slabs.

The online qualifier does the filtering. Anyone who signs up plays. The top 16 fly or drive to Miami. That structure keeps the field honest without turning the event into a pure invitation list. People who have been grinding decks in the digital beta or at local pop-outs get a path. People who only show up for the yacht still get the party. The table is separate from the after-party on purpose.

## From free mint to felt

Rise of the Pack is 111 hand-drawn cards. Five classes. Forty-card decks. Forty HP heroes. The rules live at ddltcg.com. The digital version has been playable. The physical boxes have been arriving. Community members have been posting master-set progress, full-art concepts, and AGS graded slabs. One account even mapped a Pop Report Galaxy of real graded copies.

None of that is required to understand the championship. Two people can sit across a table with two decks and play. The online qualifier just selects who sits across the table in Miami. The same pipeline that turned Dogecoin inscriptions into plush, then into cards, now turns cards into a scored event with a champion.

Most collections stop at the drop. A few make it to merch. Fewer still put a real competitive format in a real venue with the product as the prize. The championship is the next concrete room after the DDNYC tournament. It is not a recap. It is the calendar moving forward.

## Why the room still matters

Daily Spaces built the culture. DDNYC proved the culture could fill a hotel. The championship asks a narrower question: can the game hold a bracket. The answer does not require a keynote. It requires 16 decks, a timer, and a table that does not get packed up at 3 p.m. for a panel.

Pack members who have been trading on eBay, grading through AGS and Collectors Club, and finishing master sets already treat the set like it has a second life. The championship gives that second life a scoreboard. Someone will leave with the Ultra Rare Gary. Someone will leave with ten boxes. The rest of the field will leave knowing exactly which decks beat theirs.

That is the free-mint thesis extended one more step. Give the culture away at the start. Build the object that can leave the chain. Then put the object on a table and let the pack decide who is best with it. No outside capital required. The team has been self-funding the rooms. The championship is another room.

## What we do with the next six weeks

If you have cards, build the deck you want to run. The online round-robin is the filter. If you are still opening boxes, the secondary market is open and the search term is still doginal dogs. If you are heading to Miami anyway, the Rip & Sip block now has a tournament attached. Show up ready or show up to watch. Both count. Only one gets the title.

The dates and exact prize wording live on the official posts. Confirm them before you book. The direction does not require confirmation. The first official championship is on the calendar. The table is moving to Miami.

Proof the decks. Lock the flights. Keep the felt open.

See you in the next room.
