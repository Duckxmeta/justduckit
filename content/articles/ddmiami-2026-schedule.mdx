The schedule landed. Not a teaser. The full map.

DDMIAMI 2026 runs December 7 to 9. Higher Ground and the Rooftop at Arlo Hotel. Collectors Club in Wynwood for the cards. The Vessel on Biscayne Bay for the close. Swag drop, Dog Pod studio, VIP dinners, rooftop kickoff, Rip & Sip that doubles as the DDL Miami Championship, Dogs at Sea, then the Hangover Hangout.

Bark called it the most ambitious yet. We already know the pattern from Chelsea and Vegas. The rooms get bigger. The product gets more physical. The pack still has to show up.

## Day one starts with something you can hold

December 7 opens at Higher Ground at Arlo Hotel. Swag Drop and Welcome Party, 10AM to 5PM. That is the entry. Merch first. Faces second. The same sequence that worked in New York: something wearable before the agenda starts speaking.

New this year is Dog Pod. An in-person production studio inside the welcome block. Creators can film on site. No more “we will catch it later.” The content happens in the room while the room is still open.

VIP Dinner at 7PM. Then the Kick-Off moves upstairs to the Rooftop at Arlo, 8PM to 11PM. Open air. Miami skyline. The first night is the handshake. Nobody needs a keynote to know whether the density is real.

## The championship is the point of day two

December 8 is the card day. Rip & Sip at Collectors Club, 11AM to 3PM. That session is also the DDL Miami Championship. In-person tournament for Doginal Dogs Legends. Prizes on the line. Players on tables. Everyone else ripping packs and watching the matches.

Collectors Club is the right building. Card shop energy. Grading capability on site. High-end collectors already walk through those doors. The first official championship of this size does not happen in a hotel ballroom. It happens where the cardboard already lives.

Rise of the Pack left the screen at DDNYC. Preorders shipped. Packs hit doorsteps. Secondary listings thickened. Now the same cards get a public stage in Miami. The people who built decks in Spaces and group chats get a reason to bring the best version. One Legend rules the table that day.

VIP Dinner follows at 7PM. Then Dogs at Sea. Boarding on The Vessel from 7PM to 8PM. Voyage runs 8PM to midnight. Four hours on Biscayne Bay with the whole community. Luxury floating venue that has hosted real events before this one. The close is not another rooftop. It is water.

## Day three is the honest hangover

December 9 is Hangover Hangout, 9AM to 12PM, back at Higher Ground. Coffee. Recovery. One last morning before the flights. Events usually dissolve here into “we should do this again.” The schedule puts a name on the recovery instead of pretending the weekend ends at midnight.

Three days. One city. The venues are stacked on purpose. Arlo as home base. Collectors Club for the game. The Vessel for the finale. Hospitality treated as product, not backdrop.

## What the schedule is actually saying

Most projects announce a city and a date and hope the culture fills the gap. This one publishes the rooms. Swag window. Creator studio. Championship bracket. Yacht boarding times. Hangover block. The map is public. The only variable left is who gets on the plane.

Tickets and swag moved fast. That is not a press-release flex. It is the same signal we saw before Chelsea: the pack already decided the room was worth the ticket before the full agenda dropped. The championship gives the card community a concrete reason. The yacht gives everyone else a close that does not look like every other crypto afterparty.

We do not need to invent sold-out percentages or claim the championship will set records. The published agenda is enough. A live tournament for a hand-drawn TCG that started as Dogecoin inscriptions is the pipeline working in public. Free mint to IRL to tabletop to championship stage. The object travels. The community decides what it is worth by showing up and playing.

## The assignment is the same one

Daily Spaces built the culture. DDNYC proved the rooms could fill. DDMIAMI is the next receipt. If the decks are real, bring them. If the friendships are real, book the flight. If the product only exists in the replies, the championship will still happen without you.

The pack has been counting the days. The schedule just made the count specific.
