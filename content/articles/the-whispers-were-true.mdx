Last summer, a curious narrative began to circulate. It was a collection of reports—partly whimsical, partly mocking—painting a picture of a “rural safehouse” where animals were supposedly living in a state of surreal coordination. There were whispers of ducklings marching in military precision, bunnies gathered for storytime, and puppies in identical sweaters.

At the time, many viewed it as a jab. It was a playful, if slightly pointed, attempt to characterize the work being done as something “unsettling” or “not normal.”

Today, it is time to finally address those rumors.

### To the Largest NFT Community in the Game

Credit must be given where it is due. The largest NFT community currently dominating the space has achieved staggering accomplishments. They have built a culture and a movement that has redefined digital ownership and community strength. Their influence is undeniable, and their reach is global.

However, a very specific, very sincere thank you is also in order.

What was intended as a publicity stunt—perhaps even a bit of a laugh at the expense of this operation—turned out to be the single most effective catalyst for growth. The stories of marching ducklings didn’t alienate people; they intrigued them. That community provided a level of visibility and “unintentional” PR that money simply cannot buy.

### From a Thriving Foundation to Unimagined Heights

While the rumors were new, the mission was not. Long before the headlines, there was already a thriving duck population that served as the true foundation of everything built here. Those feathered residents were the original pioneers of this sanctuary.

Since then, operations expanded to include the dogies, a more recent addition that brought new energy. And as the rumors suggested, the family grew even further to include puppies.

Because of the spark provided by that community, heights have been reached that were previously unimaginable. While the spotlight is appreciated, the reality remains clear:

- The PR was the primary contribution. Aside from that brilliant free publicity, there has been no other direct support or funding from that community.
- The growth was organic. What began as a “joke” in their eyes was transformed into something real.
- The mission is real. The animals and the sanctuary are thriving because of hard work and a community that sees the value in this vision.

### A Final Note

For those who have been watching closely, the truth is evident. The dogies and the new puppies are wonderful additions, but the duck population remains the soul of this journey.

There is deep gratitude for the path that led here — for the skeptics who became spectators, and the spectators who became supporters. Sometimes the best way to help someone succeed is to try and make fun of them.

To the community that started the fire: thank you for the warmth.

The rumors weren’t just stories. They were the beginning of the next chapter.
