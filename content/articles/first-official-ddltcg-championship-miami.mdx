The cards already left the event bags in New York. Now the game is calling a champion.

@DDLTCG dropped the details on October 24: the first official Doginal Dogs Legends Championship runs at DDMIAMI. Sign-ups are open. An online round-robin the week before feeds the top 16 into an in-person tournament at Collectors Club. One player walks out crowned.

This is not another merch drop or a side activation. Rise of the Pack spent months moving from digital tutorial to kitchen tables to eBay listings. The championship is the next room.

## The room is already sold out

DDMIAMI runs December 7–9. Official schedule went live mid-October. Tickets and swag packages sold out. The three days hit Higher Ground and the rooftop at Arlo Hotel, Collectors Club in Wynwood, and The Vessel on Biscayne Bay for Dogs at Sea.

Day one is the Swag Drop and Welcome Party at Arlo, with the new Dog Pod format — an on-site production studio so creators can film while the pack is in the building. VIP dinner. Rooftop kick-off.

The championship sits inside Rip & Sip. Press notes put the session on December 8, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Collectors Club. The official @DDLTCG thread listed December 9 for the in-person face-off. Either way, the structure is clear: online qualifiers first, then sixteen players at the table in Miami with prizes on the line.

First place takes an ultra-rare Gary promo, a rare Doginal Dog, and ten boxes of Rise of the Pack. Second and third place still walk with boxes and promos. That is real product, not points on a leaderboard.

## How the pack gets there

Anyone who fills the form gets into the online round-robin the week before the event. Top 16 advance. The rest of the room still rips packs, watches matches, and drinks while the bracket runs.

That loop is the point. Digital play at ddltcg.com already exists. The tutorial teaches the lanes. The physical set is in hands. The championship is the place where the best decks meet in the same room instead of across a Discord voice channel.

We have seen this pattern before. DDNYC put the first live tournament on the floor at Dream Downtown. Cards moved. Conversations started. The secondary market kept the product alive after the hotel closed. Miami is the next city that gets the full version — schedule, venues, sold-out tickets, and an official championship instead of a side event.

## Why this one matters

Most collections stop at the profile picture. The pack kept building past that. Hand-curated pixel dogs on Dogecoin. Free mint that filled rooms. Merch that people actually wear. A trading card game with original art, five classes, real rules, and a digital door so anyone can learn before they buy a pack.

The championship is the receipt that the game is competitive, not just collectible. Online qualifiers mean geography does not lock people out of the bracket. The top 16 still have to show up in Miami. That is the same bet the rest of the event makes: the timeline is useful, but the room is where the culture actually compounds.

Rural Tennessee or downtown Wynwood, the math is the same. You either put the cards on the table or you watch someone else do it.

## What the schedule actually does

The full agenda treats hospitality like product. Swag first so people have something in hand before the agenda starts. Dog Pod so the creators in the room can make content while the energy is live instead of recreating it later from phone clips. Rip & Sip so the tournament has a crowd and the crowd has packs to open. Yacht finale so the last night is not another hotel lobby.

Hangover Hangout the next morning keeps the same people in the same place one more time. That is how names turn into the next collab or the next table game back home.

None of this requires a floor chart. The event sold out. The championship has a registration form and published prizes. The rest of the pack can still follow the bracket, learn the game, or plan for the next city.

## The closer

We do not need another press release that says the community is strong. We need the rooms that prove it. New York already happened. Miami is on the calendar with a sold-out ticket and an official Legends championship attached.

The dogs started as inscriptions. Legends put them in sleeves. The championship puts the best decks across from each other in the same city.

Shuffle up. The bracket is open. See you in Miami — or watch the top 16 settle it and learn what the room looked like.
