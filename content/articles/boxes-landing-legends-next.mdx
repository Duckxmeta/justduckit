The boxes are hitting doorsteps.

@DDLTCG confirmed the preorders have all been shipped and are currently being received. Community posts show sealed boxes arriving early, people posting the packaging, and the quiet satisfaction of something that left the screen and landed in a kitchen. Booster packs are available at the shop for anyone still hunting singles or starter volume. That is not a soft launch. That is fulfillment.

The same week the cardboard arrives, two more layers turned on. AGS grading is officially fully live for Doginal Dogs Legends. Holders can send cards in. The digital beta is free to play in the browser — 40-card decks, five classes, bot matches, PvP, daily tournaments. And the first official DDLTCG Championship is locked for December at DDMIAMI. One Legend will rule them all.

We spent nearly two years drawing the 111 cards by hand. The free mint on Dogecoin was the start. The kitchen table is the point.

## What actually arrived

Preorder boxes contain 24 packs. Every card is original hand-drawn artwork from the core team. No generative filler. The set is Rise of the Pack — the origin edition. Classes run Wizard, Crown, Zombie, Bow, Pirate. Neutrals fill the gaps. Three copies max. Forty life. Five lanes. Ten mana. Drop the other Hero to zero.

People who ordered are opening them with kids, posting pulls, and already talking about keeping one sealed. That is collector behavior, not timeline behavior. The same culture that inscribed 10,000 pixel dogs for free is now treating the physical extension like something worth protecting and trading.

AGS grading going live is the next receipt. A third-party grading company taking submissions on a community TCG means the cards have enough volume and enough interest that someone outside the Spaces is willing to put a number on them. It is not a promise of value. It is proof that the object travels.

The digital side removes the last excuse. Anyone can load ddltcg.com/play, fight a bot, and learn the lanes before the box even arrives. Same rules. Same win condition. Two doors into the same game.

## The rooms that are already booked

While the mail is still moving, the next room is on the calendar. DDMIAMI runs in December. The official account posted the 60-day mark. Tickets and packages have been live. The championship announcement sits on top of that — the first official DDLTCG event of its kind, hosted inside the larger Miami takeover.

That is the loop the pack has been running since the free mint: on-chain identity, physical product, live room. No one is waiting for a market candle to decide whether the next table exists. The tables are already reserved.

Yesterday the official account noted it has been one year since 10/10. Most people have not survived since then. The Doginal Dogs holders are still here. The post landed with the usual replies — we are not going anywhere, we are just getting started. The product shipping this week is the concrete version of that sentence. Survival is not a vibe. It is boxes in the mail and a championship on the calendar.

Most NFT cycles peak at the mint or the drop and then go quiet. This one keeps adding layers. Free mint on Dogecoin. Self-funded events. A two-year hand-drawn TCG that sold out preorder in a day, debuted live at DDNYC, and is now arriving, gradeable, and playable digitally while the next flagship event is already sold.

Secondary markets showed the cards moving after New York. Grading shows outsiders are willing to slab them. Digital play shows the game does not require the physical box to start. Championship structure shows there is a competitive path beyond kitchen tables. None of those steps require inventing numbers or claiming we watched every package land. The official posts exist. The shop links exist. The event page exists.

Rural Tennessee still hosts Spaces and side conversations. New York and Miami host the big rooms. The cards themselves do not care where they are opened. That is the product.
