The official account posted it plain on November 23: DDMIAMI 2026 is in two weeks. Palm trees, Vice City, the link to the events page. No long thread. Just the date and the fact that the Dogs are about to take it.

That is the move. Not a teaser. A clock.

## The shop is the first room

Black Friday opened the full shop on November 20. Everything that still exists is up one last time. Limited stock. While supplies last. The official post put the Santa Gary promo card on the table for carts over $299. Not sold separately. Free with the qualifying order. While supplies last.

Pack members started posting the hoodies and the hats. Someone already noted the swag is how you spot each other in the wild, especially with Miami coming. The V1 Boxes of Awesomeness are the ones people are talking about as scarce. One of the last unopened ones got delisted. The physical pieces are not infinite.

This is the pipeline the pack actually runs. On-chain dogs, then something you can wear or open or hold. The sale is not a side quest. It is the part that shows up in rooms later. You either grab what is left or you watch the next person walk in with the piece that is already gone.

## The championship already started

The first DDLTCG Championship kicked off the week of November 22. Check-in on Discord. Swiss-style matchups through the week. Knockout rounds on Saturday night. Games viewable on the play site. The official Legends account put it out: time to find out who the real Legends are.

Rise of the Pack left the screen at DDNYC. It is still leaving it. Digital beta, physical cards, now brackets with actual prizes. The Miami version sits on the schedule for December 8 at Collectors Club during Rip & Sip. In-person. Same set. Different felt.

The digital one is the practice. The Miami one is the room. Both count because both require showing up with a deck instead of a screenshot.

## The schedule is already concrete

December 7 opens at Higher Ground at the Arlo with the swag drop and welcome. Day two is the championship block plus the VIP dinner and then Dogs at Sea on The Vessel. Day three is the hangover hangout back at Higher Ground. Primary hotel is the Mia Wynwood. Tickets and the bigger packages sold through earlier. The page still has the disclaimer that the schedule can change.

That is the ops side. Venues, times, a yacht that is not a metaphor. The pack has already done New York and Vegas. Miami is the next city that gets occupied for three days. The conversations that do not fit in a reply thread happen in those rooms. What people built this year. What worked. What did not. The ones who show up leave with names, not just another recap.

## What the last two weeks look like

The merch sale is the easy part. Click, cart, Santa Gary if the number hits. The harder part is the championship that is already running and the one that is twelve days out. Decks get built. Brackets get filled. People who have been in the Spaces and the Discord now have a physical table waiting.

Early physical items are getting harder to find. The boxes that were the first way the culture left the wallet are circulating or locked. The next ones are the ones that get picked up in Miami. The pack has always treated the wearable and the holdable as the receipt that the on-chain piece was real.

Two weeks is not long. The clock is already on the official account. The shop is open while the stock lasts. The first championship is underway. The next room is a hotel in Wynwood and a boat on the bay.

The Dogs never quit. The timeline is the reminder. The rooms are the proof.

Until Miami. Gotta pack the bag.
