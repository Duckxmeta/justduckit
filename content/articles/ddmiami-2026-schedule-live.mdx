The official DDMiami 2026 schedule went live yesterday. Not a teaser. The full lineup, day by day, with venues named and times set.

December 7 to 9. Arlo Hotel as the home base. Collectors Club for the cards. The Vessel for the close. Tickets and the Box of Awesomeness packages moved fast when they first opened. The schedule confirms what the rooms will actually be.

We have been in enough of these rooms now to know the pattern. The timeline lights up when the calendar fills in. Then the real work starts: flights, decks, the decision to show up.

## Day one: the studio and the roof

Sunday, December 7 starts at Higher Ground inside Arlo Hotel. Swag Drop and Welcome Party from 10AM to 5PM. Same opening the pack prefers. Physical product in hand before the agenda fully kicks in.

New this year is Dog Pod. An in-person production studio running during the Welcome Party. Creators can shoot content on site instead of trying to catch moments between panels. The pack already runs daily Spaces and makes its own media. Dog Pod puts a real studio inside the event so the people who document the culture do not have to leave the building to do it.

VIP dinner at 7PM. Then the Rooftop at Arlo from 8PM to 11PM for the Kick-Off. Miami skyline, open air, the first night where the usernames become faces again.

That is the same sequence Chelsea used. Swag, density, then the roof. It works because the product is the room, not the keynote.

## Day two: championship and the water

Monday is the one the card people have been waiting for. Rip & Sip at Collectors Club from 11AM to 3PM is also the DDL Miami Championship. The first major in-person tournament for Doginal Dogs Legends.

Rise of the Pack left the screen at DDNYC. Packs opened under hotel lights. Brackets ran. Now the championship stage is a known card venue in Wynwood with an on-site grading lab. Players bring decks. Everyone else rips and watches. Major prizes on the line. The game is no longer just the thing that debuted in New York. It has a next competitive room.

VIP Dinner 2 at 7PM. Then Dogs at Sea. Boarding on The Vessel from 7PM to 8PM. Sail from 8PM to midnight. Four hours on Biscayne Bay with the pack. The Vessel has hosted real Miami nights before this one. Closing on the water is not a gimmick. It is the scale the events have reached.

Collectors Club is the right building for the championship. High-end collectibles, premium lounge, same-day grading. The same people who sleeved Rise of the Pack cards after Chelsea now have a table that treats the game like product instead of novelty.

## Day three: the hangover has a name

Tuesday wraps with the Hangover Hangout back at Higher Ground, 9AM to noon. Coffee, recovery, one last morning. The same closer the pack used in New York. Events this size do not end clean. They end with people who still want to talk before the flights.

The schedule is subject to change. That is printed on the official page. The shape is already clear enough to book around.

DDNYC proved the free-mint collection could fill a TAO venue for three days. The cards proved the on-chain dogs could become physical game pieces that trade after the event. Miami is the next compound.

Dog Pod puts the creators who already make the content inside the building instead of outside it. The DDL Championship gives the TCG a competitive stage that is not a side table in a hotel. Dogs at Sea puts the whole pack on a venue that has hosted music-week dinners and fashion runways. None of that is required for a pixel-dog project. All of it is what the pack chose to build next.

Cards turned the identity into something you can hold and trade without a wallet open.

Miami is not a victory lap. It is the next operating system. A creator studio, a championship bracket, and a yacht that requires people to actually show up. The schedule is the map. The work is still the plane ticket and the deck.

We do not need to invent attendance numbers or claim the yacht is already full. The official post went up yesterday. The community reacted in the same window. The venues are named. The championship is listed. That is enough to know the next room exists.
