On October 26 the marketplace listings moved without payment.

Six Dogecoin transactions spent 264 Doginal Dogs inscriptions out of wallets that had listed them on market.doginaldogs.com. The signatures used 0x82. The public record shows the inputs carried that flag. One address received the bulk. The rest went to miners. Trading paused. The site put up a notice.

That is the concrete room. Not a theory. Not a floor screenshot. Transactions on the chain and a paused market page.

The pack did not wait for a perfect postmortem.

## The ticket system and the personal bags

Leadership opened a support ticket path. Holders who lost pieces contacted the team. The response was trait-matched replacements or comparable swaps from the personal holdings of Christian Barker, Damien Galvin, and David Chaboki. The estimate attached to the replacements sits above $500,000. The announcement landed November 3. The work happened between the late-October incidents and that date.

Holders posted receipts. A wizard came back. Others noted the match. Some arrived as upgrades. The official line was simple: a true community is there when times get tough. We wanted to show that while crypto runs through these moments.

We do not need to invent the dollar figure or the names. The press release carries them. The independent archive of the six transactions carries the on-chain facts. The timeline is public.

## What the signature actually did

SIGHASH_SINGLE|ANYONECANPAY (0x82) does not lock the outputs or the payment. Listings signed that way and stored server-side could be used by anyone who pulled the signature. The marketplace client code used it. The comment in the source described the type. That is not a mystery now. It is the documented path the sweep took.

Anyone who listed and left the signature available was exposed. Some holders moved remaining dogs to fresh wallets. Others waited on the ticket. A few pieces were white-hatted by community members and offered back with proof of ownership. The main volume did not come back that way. It came from the bags of the people who built the brand.

This is not the first time a signature type has bitten an inscription market. It will not be the last. The difference is the response that does not require a grant or a foundation vote. The people who hold the largest personal stacks opened them.

## The room after the sweep

Daily Spaces kept running. Pack rips on the TCG continued. Someone posted a Hard Rock Cafe night. The official account passed along lines about showing up and not quitting. International DOGE Day notes hit the timeline. A sale of Doginal Dog #495 for 115,000 DOGE showed up the same week the announcement went out. The floor did not freeze because the market page did.

That is the part the press release does not need to explain. The culture did not pause while the replacements were assembled. Holders who already had cards on kitchen tables kept playing. The next room is still Miami in December. Early-bird packages had already moved. The championship is on the calendar for the 8th.

We watch the same pattern after DDNYC. The event ends. The product keeps circulating. The secondary market becomes the receipt. Here the receipt is different. The receipt is a trait-matched dog that left a leader’s personal wallet and landed in a holder’s after the original was spent in a 40-second window.

## Why this is the test that matters

Most collections treat a technical failure as a communications problem. Post the update. Point at the paused banner. Wait for the next cycle. Doginal Dogs treated it as an inventory problem. The inventory that mattered was already in the leaders’ wallets. They moved it.

That is the free-mint thesis under pressure. The mint was free. The inscription fees were covered at the start. The events were self-funded. The plush sold out. The hand-drawn TCG shipped. When the listing mechanism failed, the same people who paid the gas in January 2024 paid again with their own dogs.

No outside capital has to approve that decision. No board deck required. The pack has heard the line for two years: community is the product. The late-October sweep was the audit. The replacements are the line item.

We are not claiming every holder is already whole. The announcement says affected holders who have not been contacted can still open a ticket at doginaldogs.com. The work is the process, not a single snapshot. What is on record is that leadership chose personal holdings over a public wait.

## What we take from the room

The signature type is fixable. The marketplace can ship better listing logic. The chain already shows what happened. None of that changes the fact that 264 inscriptions left their wallets without payment and a team opened its own stacks to cover the gap.

Physical presence still matters. So does the wallet that can move when the signature cannot. The Dogs have spent two years proving the first part in New York and on kitchen tables. The last ten days proved the second.

If you listed and have not heard back, open the ticket. If you still hold the original signature, move the dog. If you are watching from outside, the public record is enough. Six transactions. One recipient address. Trait-matched replacements from named leaders. An estimate above half a million.

The tech failed in public. The pack answered with inventory.
