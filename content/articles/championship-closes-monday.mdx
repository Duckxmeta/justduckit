Registration for the first official DDL TCG Championship closes Monday at midnight Eastern. Top 16 advance to the live finals at DD Miami. The pack is already talking about flights, canoes, and which deck to bring.

Twenty-three days until the rooms in Miami open. The cards left Chelsea in September. They did not stop moving. Now the table has a date, a venue, and a bracket that requires a name on a list.

## The list closes Monday

@DDLTCG posted the registration thread on November 10. Every participant gets one exclusive Giga Rare Gary promo card. Entries are limited to one per buyer. The shop link is the door. As of this writing the product page shows sold out or low stock — verify the exact cutoff yourself before you treat the window as open.

The format is simple on paper. Online round-robin the week before. Top 16 fly or drive to Collectors Club in Wynwood on December 8 for the in-person final during Rip & Sip, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Prizes include rare Gary promos, a Doginal Dog, and Rise of the Pack boxes. That is the official line from the account and the community posts that quoted it.

We do not invent the prize list. We notice the list exists and the clock is running.

## Concrete rooms, not another Space

DD Miami runs December 7–9. Official schedule page is live on doginaldogs.com. Day one at Higher Ground / Arlo Hotel: swag drop and welcome, VIP dinner, rooftop kick-off. Day two is the championship table at Collectors Club, then Dogs at Sea on The Vessel in Biscayne Bay. Day three is the hangover hangout back at Arlo. Schedule is marked subject to change. Dog Pod, the on-site creator studio, sits inside the welcome block.

These are not abstract venues. Arlo Wynwood is the recommended hotel. Collectors Club already partners with AGS for same-day grading. The yacht is a floating room that opened last year. The pack has been posting about driving the whole way if flights cancel. One account said they will bring a canoe. Another is stockpiling toploaders and snacks. That is the energy between the last room and the next one.

The official account dropped a short video this morning: “The Doginal Dogs never quit. Keep showing up, keep trying new things, and keep putting in that work.” Six frames of the pack. No roadmap slide. Just the reminder while the championship list is still open.

Daily Spaces keep running. Vee, Chief Roar Officer, had one live this morning under the CSN banner. The pack taps in the same way it has for months. The difference is the calendar now has a physical address and a bracket that rewards the people who already registered.

## What the free mint actually bought

The dogs started as a free mint on Dogecoin in January 2024. No raise. No paid influencers. The inscriptions sat on the chain. Then the hand-drawn cards left the screen at DDNYC. Preorder sold out. Packs ripped under hotel lights. Secondary market listings appeared on eBay the same week. Now the first official championship has a registration window that closes in days and finals in the same city that already hosted Art Basel week energy.

That sequence is the pipeline. On-chain identity becomes a physical object becomes a game people will travel for. The championship is not a side quest. It is the receipt that the table has enough players who treat the cards as real product.

Holders who already have boxes are deciding whether to grade the hits at Collectors Club on site or sleeve them for the bracket. Community posts show people opening packs at local events and building master sets page by page. One builder is laminating pages of a custom binder. The work continues between the big rooms.

## Why Monday matters more than the floor

Most collections talk about the next event until the tickets sell out and then go quiet. This one posts the schedule, opens the championship door, and keeps the daily Spaces running while people argue about which class to main. Registration closing is the moment the abstract “see you in Miami” turns into a name on a list or a seat that stays empty.

We do not claim we counted every entry. We can see the thread, the shop product, the official event page, and the pack posts about getting there anyway. That is enough to know the room is real.

If you are still deciding, the window is measured in days, not weeks. If you already registered, the online round-robin is the next concrete step. If you are just watching the Spaces, the championship is the part that cannot be attended from a phone.

## The closer

The dogs never quit because the people who hold them keep putting the work in the calendar. Monday closes the list. December 8 opens the table at Collectors Club. The yacht is after that. The hangover hangout is the morning after.

Show up for the bracket or show up for the rooms. Either way the pack is already packing.
