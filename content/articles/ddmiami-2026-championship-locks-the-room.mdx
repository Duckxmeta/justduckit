The schedule dropped. The tickets were already gone.

DDMIAMI 2026 runs December 7 through 9. Full agenda is public. Higher Ground at the Arlo Hotel for the open. Collectors Club in Wynwood for the main event. A floating venue on Biscayne Bay for the close. Tickets and the Box of Awesomeness packages sold out before most people finished reading the press note.

That is the pattern we already know. DDNYC filled a TAO hotel. Vegas filled rooms. Miami is next on the calendar and the same people who showed up in Chelsea already bought the pass.

The difference this time is the table.

## The championship is the product

Day two is Rip & Sip at Collectors Club, 11AM to 3PM. That session is also the DDL Miami Championship. In-person tournament for Doginal Dogs Legends. Prizes on the line. Cards that left New York in September now have a second room that treats them like a game instead of a souvenir.

We watched the packs hit tables under the Dream Downtown lights. We watched the first tournament run with a Doginal Dog in the prize mix. We watched the secondary market open the week after and keep the cardboard moving. The championship in Miami is the next proof that the hand-drawn set was never only for the people who already flew to New York.

Collectors Club is not a hotel ballroom rented for a night. It is a card room. The pack is booking the kind of space that already knows what a playset looks like. That choice is the signal. Legends is not merch that happens to have rules. It is a game that happens to come from inscriptions on Dogecoin.

## The rest of the block is still hospitality

December 7 opens at Higher Ground with the Swag Drop and Welcome Party, 10AM to 5PM. Same move as Chelsea: something you can hold before the agenda starts. A new creator studio format sits inside the welcome block. The Dog Pod. People who already make the daily Spaces and the morning radio now get a room built for that work instead of borrowing a corner of the hotel.

Night one has the VIP dinner and the rooftop kick-off. Night two, after the championship, is Dogs at Sea on The Vessel. Boarding 7PM to 8PM, voyage 8PM to midnight. The floating venue is the closer. Same instinct as the PHD roof: put the pack somewhere the city cannot ignore and let the conversations run.

December 9 is the Hangover Hangout back at Higher Ground, 9AM to noon. The block that always tells you whether the week actually landed. Names get written down. Follow-ups get promised. Most of those promises die in the group chat. The ones that do not are how the next collab or the next table gets built.

## What the sold-out actually measures

Floor sits around 47k DOGE. Grails still move. Adam Weitsman swept the diamond and the wizard in the same afternoon earlier this month and the timeline noticed. None of that is the story this week. The story is the same group of people who already own the dogs and the cards decided the next room was worth the ticket before the full agenda even existed.

That is the free-mint thesis still working. Give the culture away at the start. Keep showing up in physical space. Let the product compound. The championship does not require a new whitepaper. It requires people who already know how to sleeve a card and sit across from each other for three hours.

We do not need to invent attendance numbers or claim the yacht is already full of grails. The public facts are enough. Schedule is live. Tickets are sold out. The first in-person Legends championship is on the books in a room that already speaks the language of the game. The pack that filled Chelsea is booking Miami the same way.

## The work between now and December

Daily tournaments are already running on the brackets site. Academy sessions keep teaching the rules. Packs keep getting ripped on Sundays. The championship in Wynwood is not a debut. It is the next public table after the one that already happened in New York.

If you have cards, the secondary is still open and the search term is still obvious. If you are building decks, the rules page is public and the digital beta is already playable. If you are heading to Miami, the same energy that turned a hotel into a clubhouse for three days is now pointed at a card room and a boat.

The rooms keep opening because the people in them treat showing up as the product. Inscriptions started it. Hand-drawn cards extended it. A championship in December is just the next place the table sits.
