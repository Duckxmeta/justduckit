The timeline got quiet, then it got clear.

On October 26, six Dogecoin transactions spent 264 Doginal Dogs inscriptions that had been listed on the official marketplace. The signatures used a sighash that did not lock the outputs. The dogs left the wallets that listed them. Trading on market.doginaldogs.com paused. The notice is still up.

What followed is the part the pack is living.

## The room after the sweep

Holders opened tickets in Discord. They posted the losses. Some dogs went to one address. Some rode miner fees out of the transactions. The on-chain record is public. The independent archive at doginaldogs.info lists the six transactions, the 0x82 signatures, the blocks, and the counts. Radar trackers on DogeFeed show the same sweep inputs and the same primary wallet.

No one needed a press release to know what happened. The inscriptions were gone from the listing wallets. The marketplace was paused. The question was what the people who built the pack would do with the list of affected holders.

## From our own holdings

The team started matching replacements from collections it already held. Trait-matched where it could. Fair swaps. In some cases upgrades. Holders who lost pieces said a personal message from someone on the team made the wait manageable. Tickets kept moving through Sunday. Replacements started landing.

Bark posted on November 1: Trust the process. Why are hard times necessary? Because they show you exactly who's really with you. Doginal Dogs = Family.

Shield amplified it. Vee put out a direct shout-out to Bark, Shield, GodsBurnt, and the crew for how they moved after the marketplace issue. AimDontMiss posted gratitude for a solution and a ticket already submitted for a dognapped dog. BooMetaX shared a photo and the same line: hard times show who's really with you. Truck, TwinTowerCity, and others noted the scale of covering from the team's own assets and the rarer pieces going out in some cases.

None of that is a slogan. It is the sequence of posts and the Discord process still running.

## What the numbers do and do not say

Public estimates of the value range. One TechBullion piece put the team's coverage at an estimated $500,000-plus in assets. The independent archive gives a wider band depending on the price basis used for the 264 inscriptions. We are not treating either figure as a closed ledger. The point that is already on the timeline is simpler: the replacements are coming from holdings the team already owned, not from a new mint or a buyer-funded pool. Holders are not being asked to repurchase the dogs they listed.

The marketplace remains paused while protections are upgraded. The site notice cites AI-driven exploits across the internet as the reason for the temporary halt. View and share still work. Listings do not.

## The pack that already exists

This is the same community that sold out Rise of the Pack preorders, filled DDNYC, and started organizing its own Legends tournaments in Charlotte and elsewhere. The same people who grade cards, build master sets, and show up for charity auctions that feed the Do Only Good Everyday foundation. The exploit did not invent the pack. It tested whether the people who already treat the dogs as family would treat the losses the same way.

Hard times do not create the family. They reveal it. Bark's line is not new language for this group. It is the language the group already uses when the rooms get real. The personal messages, the Discord tickets, the trait matches, the upgrades — those are the receipts that the line is operational.

We do not need to invent that every holder is already made whole. The process is ongoing. Some tickets are still open. Some holders are still waiting their turn. The posts of thanks are real. The posts asking for patience are also real. Both belong in the same account of what the last week looked like.

## Why this is the story today

Crypto has plenty of incidents and plenty of statements. What moves here is the difference between a statement and a replacement dog that matches the traits of the one that left. The difference between a paused marketplace and a personal note that a specific case was read. The difference between a community that talks about family and one that opens its own holdings when the listings get spent without payment.

The dogs are inscribed on Dogecoin. They stay on the chain whether the marketplace is open or not. The people who hold them are deciding, in public, what the pack does when the signatures that should have protected a listing do the opposite. Right now the decision looks like tickets, matches, and the same sentence Bark posted on Sunday.

Trust the process. Hard times show who is with you. Doginal Dogs = Family.

That is not a closer. That is the current state of the room.

Proof the tickets. Match the traits. Keep the replacements moving.

The table is still open.
