Registration is officially open.

That is the line @DDLTCG dropped today. If you bought the Victorious Gary promo card, you are eligible for the first official Doginal Dogs Legends Championship. The window is 48 hours. Fill the form. Join the Discord. After that closes, the schedule drops.

This is not another announcement about packs shipping. This is the step where the cards leave the binder and enter a bracket.

## The room before the room

The path is already public. Participants who lock in run an online round-robin the week before the event. Top 16 advance to the live face-off. That face-off is December 9, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Collectors Club in Miami, during DD Miami. Rips & Sips. One champion.

Every participant who makes it through registration receives one exclusive Giga Rare Gary promo. The championship itself carries bigger stakes: Ultra Rare Gary promos, a rare Doginal Dog, and stacks of Rise of the Pack boxes for the top spots. Those details have been circulating since the initial announcement. What changed today is the door is open for the people who already paid the entry via the promo.

The form is the filter. Discord is the table where the bracket talk starts. Forty-eight hours is not a lot of time if you have been sitting on the promo and waiting for the exact link. It is plenty if you already knew this was the next move.

## From free mint to felt

The dogs started as 10,000 pixel pieces inscribed on Dogecoin. Free mint. No capital raise. The community carried it. Then Legends arrived as a real trading card game: 111 hand-drawn cards, five classes, actual rules, physical packs that sold out and hit tables at DDNYC.

DDNYC proved the product could leave the screen. Packs in hands. First live tournament. Cards on felt. People arguing pulls in a hotel that had turned into a clubhouse for three days. The secondary market kept moving after everyone flew home. That was the receipt that Rise of the Pack is not merch that dies when the event ends.

Now the same set has an official championship path. Online qualifier first, then a specific room in Miami with a start time and an end time. Collectors Club. Same venue that has been grading cards on-site. The pack that showed up for the hotel in New York is being asked to show up for the form tonight and the table in December.

This is the pipeline the brand has been running: on-chain inscription to physical product to in-person rooms. The championship is the next concrete room.

## Who actually fills the bracket

Most NFT projects treat tournaments like content. Post the graphic, run a one-off Discord event, move on. The Dogs have been running daily Spaces for a long stretch. The TCG has been running live play at events and local tables. The championship is the first time the official account is locking a structured path with entry via a specific promo card, an online stage, and a hard cutoff for top 16 IRL.

The people who bought the Victorious Gary promo already made a choice. Registration is the next choice. Form and Discord are not hard. They are the difference between owning a promo and being on the schedule.

We have watched the same pattern at every step. The free mint filled because people showed up. The preorder sold out because people bought. DDNYC filled a TAO hotel because people booked the ticket and got on the plane. The championship will fill the same way. Not because a floor looks healthy. Because enough of the pack treats the form like the next room they need to be in.

Rural Tennessee or downtown Miami does not matter as much as whether the person with the promo fills the form before the 48 hours close. The pack is the people who keep doing the next concrete thing.

## What the next 48 hours decide

After registration closes, the schedule posts. Then the online round-robin runs the week leading into Miami. Top 16 get the IRL seat. Everyone else watches the bracket or shows up for the rest of DD Miami — the swag, the Dog Pod, the nights on the water.

The championship is not the whole event. It is the part where the cards that left the screen at DDNYC get a crown. One player walks out with the Ultra Rare Gary, the rare dog, and the boxes. The rest of the field still gets the Giga Rare promo for entering. The table still happens.

That is the difference between a collection that posts floor screenshots and a pack that builds rooms. The form is open right now. The Discord is open. The schedule comes after. The table is already booked in Miami.

If you hold the promo, the next move is not another Space or another pull video. It is the form.
